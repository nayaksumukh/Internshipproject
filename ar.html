Udupi is a coastal town in the Indian state of Karnataka and is famous for its beaches, temples, and delicious South Indian cuisine. Some popular tourist attractions in Udupi include:
St. Mary's Island - a group of islands located off the coast of Udupi, known for its unique geological formations and crystal clear waters.
Sri Krishna Temple - a famous Hindu temple dedicated to Lord Krishna, and one of the seven Mukti Sthalas in the region.
Malpe Beach - a scenic beach located near Udupi, popular for its water sports activities and beautiful sunsets.
Manipal - a nearby university town known for its educational institutions and bustling student culture.
Udupi Sri Krishna Matha - a Hindu monastery that serves as the headquarters of the Ashta Mathas, the eight monasteries that run the administration of the Sri Krishna Temple.
Kodi Beach - another picturesque beach located near Udupi, known for its serene surroundings and breathtaking views.
Kapu Beach - a serene and secluded beach located near Udupi, popular for its unspoiled natural beauty and serene surroundings.
