Welcome to Cricket Drive, where young dreams take shape!


Our Journey

Established in 2019, driven by a passion to address a critical gap in the cricket landscape: true grassroots development. Our founder and leadership team, comprising of experienced & certified coaches and cricket enthusiasts, remain committed to achieving a breakthrough moment. Despite progress, diversification within the cricket community remains in its infancy, relying on support from local towns and schools to propel the sport forward. Cricket Drive is dedicated to overcoming these challenges through innovative programs that streamline the youth pathway, fostering a brighter future for the game we love.


Our Mission

Nurture the next generation of cricket champions, empowering them to reach their full potential. We're dedicated to creating a supportive ecosystem that fosters growth, learning, and success.


Our Vision

Produce confident, skilled, and dedicated cricketers who make a positive impact on and off the field. Join us on this exciting journey, and let's drive the future of cricket together!


At Cricket Drive, we're passionate about

  • Developing young talent through expert coaching and mentorship
  • Building character and sportsmanship through teamwork and discipline
  • Inspiring a love for the game that lasts a lifetime

Cricket Drive Junior Pathway


What we do?


  • Develop their cricketing skills: Batting, bowling, fielding, and overall game awareness.
  • Build teamwork and sportsmanship: Learn to collaborate effectively and develop a positive sporting attitude.
  • Gain valuable life skills: Discipline, dedication, and the ability to set and achieve goals.

Benefits for young athletes:

  • Structured Coaching: Our qualified coaches provide personalized training plans and skill development programs.
  • Competitive Matches: Participation in league matches allows players to test their skills, gain experience, and build confidence.
  • Pathway to Excellence: We offer opportunities for players to progress through the ranks and potentially pursue professional careers.
  • Positive Learning Environment: We foster a supportive and inclusive environment where players can learn, develop, and have fun.


Junior League Cricket(JLC)

Cricket Drive is the founding members of this league. Our affiliation with a recognized cricket league ensures competitive matches and exposure to a wider cricketing network. With teams across GA, Florida, TN, Maryland and VA, this is a great platform to have your kids play D1 and D2 cricket.



USA Cricket Youth Pathway(NC HUB)

Being part of the USA Cricket Youth Pathway program allows our players to access national coaching resources and development opportunities. Cricket Drive is one of the committee members on the NC Hub, and strives to help pool skilled and talented kids across U11, U13, U15, U17 and U19 to provide a platform/path of them to play regionals, zonals & Nationals every year.



Gladiators-Youth Franchise

This affiliation provides access to training methods, mentorship, and potential pathways to professional cricket. This franchise helps the cream of the crop youth to represent in professional leagues across the country, and adapt to playing with really competitive cricket.
